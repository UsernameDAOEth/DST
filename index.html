// SECTION: WHY_THIS_EXISTS

On the live event-market tape, roughly four of every five taker contracts enter below ten cents implied — and resolve near zero. The favorites side grinds pennies that fees mostly reclaim. The 20–60¢ band — where actual theses live — is nearly empty.

The middle of this market is vacant because nobody makes mid-probability discipline tractable. This system exists to fill that vacancy: structure, invalidation, and a probability basis — enforced, not suggested.

// SECTION: THE_SIGNAL_BOX

Ideas are allowed. Unaudited ideas are not.

Candidate theses come only from registered signal classes: fixed rules, named public data sources, backtests against historical resolutions, and kill criteria written before the first signal fires. Every class is Brier-scored against the hardest benchmark there is — the market price itself — and retires automatically when it stops beating it. And every output passes the same gate as a human thesis. The engine can, and will, fail its own signals. That refusal is a feature, printed on the record.

WHAT A SIGNAL IS
A pre-packaged thesis from a registered class — cross-venue divergence · reference-model gap · tail-bias fade · funding harvest — published with its probability claim, its own verdict and hash, and the class's live Brier-vs-market record.
WHAT IT IS NOT
An auto-trade. Ever. You read the signal, its audit, and its class's public record — and you decide. Execution is always yours, always your key.
THE TRUST RAMP
Shadow first: every class publishes with zero execution until its record exists in public. Classes that stop beating the market retire themselves — and the funerals are public too.
// SECTION: THE_LEDGER

Every verdict is graded at resolution. The accuracy claim is not "we predict markets" — it is filter alpha: do PASS-verdicted theses outperform the FAIL cohort they were separated from? That number is computed from hash-stamped rows written before outcomes existed. A public, tamper-evident scoreboard is the roadmap — receipts, not testimony.
